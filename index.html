    Guess the album cover in 6 tries.

    After each guess, the image zooms out revealing more of the cover.
    Play the Daily mode to compete with friends, or practice in Endless mode.
    Stuck? Click the lightbulb icon at the top left to view unlocked hints after each guess.

    About Waxdle

    Made with ♡ by Jacekarino

    This is an open-source prototype / proof of concept. The game is functional, but may have issues. Done in a day for fun and as a learning experience and a portfolio piece. Waxdle uses LastFM API for fetching the cover arts. You can definitely cheat by looking up the albums database by the way.
